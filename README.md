# anemoia
I am trying to build a premium, immersive driving experience that brings your real-world neighbourhood into a virtual game. Starting with a Dodge, the project aims to let players drive familiar local routes from the comfort of their homes, with realistic roads, recognizable surroundings, traffic, and eventually other players moving through the same virtual neighbourhood.

Unlike traditional driving games built around fictional cities or racing circuits, our vision is to recreate the feeling of driving around places people know. As the experience evolves, residents may encounter other players, see people around their neighbourhood, and interact with a virtual representation of their local environment.

The long-term vision includes multiplayer experiences, virtual reality (VR), and a community-driven platform where local businesses and residents can request to be featured. Paid business placements and in-game marketing will become part of the platform as it grows.

We are not simply building a driving game. We are building a premium virtual experience where gaming, local identity, social connection, and the real world meet.
